Help · works offline

Speak. Check. Keep writing.

Record speech locally, choose when to review it, and place the finished text at your cursor.

F1 opens Help from Settings. Ctrl + F searches this guide in your browser.

Start dictating

Open Local Dictation from the Windows notification area. Wait for Ready · Local transcription, then choose your microphone and model. Valid settings changes are saved automatically; check the saving status before starting.

Write directly at your cursor

  1. On Dictation, choose After recording → Insert directly. Enable Live transcription and Insert live text at the cursor. Valid changes save automatically.
  2. Click your destination field, with no text selected.
  3. Press and release your shortcut, Ctrl + Shift by default. Speak.
  4. Press the shortcut again, or select Finish in the banner, to stop.

Insert the result after recording

Choose After recording → Insert directly and turn off Insert live text at the cursor, then start with the shortcut from your destination field. The setting saves automatically.

Recognition continues without live cursor insertion. Finish in the original field to insert automatically; finishing elsewhere keeps the result for the small Insert action.

Record starts from the app window without an external cursor. When the text is ready, click the destination field and use Insert.

Preview before inserting

Choose After recording → Preview first. Your destination stays unchanged throughout recording and completion. Live recognition can stay on. The completed result opens Review for editing, Save text and explicit Insert. Live insertion is paused; its remembered choice returns when you choose Insert directly again.

The app inserts text; it does not send it.

Sending, submitting, and saving the destination remain your actions.

Recordings last up to 10 minutes. During a session, Esc cancels recording or verification. Cancellation or an interrupted recording does not automatically insert received text; it stays available for review.

Automatic completion belongs to Insert directly. If insertion cannot be confirmed, your text remains available in the banner. Preview first always waits for you to choose Insert, including after resolving every phrase.

Live text and the minimal banner

Live transcription recognizes speech while you record. In Insert directly with Insert live text at the cursor enabled, the app updates its own draft in your original field, including corrections after a pause. The small banner shows the recording state, model, language and elapsed time. Preview first keeps the destination unchanged.

Press Ctrl + Shift again to finish. The selected dictation model completes the original recording. Insert directly attempts final insertion after any required verification; confirmed insertion closes the banner. Preview first opens Review. Verification and word choices are optional.

In desktop Outlook, Insert directly inserts the complete result once after recording. If live delivery fails in another app, Local Dictation remembers that app and uses final-only insertion from the next recording. Live recognition remains available. Use Settings → Application compatibility → Re-enable live insertion for remembered apps to try live delivery again; Outlook stays final-only. Preview first opens Review in Outlook too.

Move the banner

Drag its waveform or status area. It remains small and does not take keyboard focus. The soft edge fades to a transparent window boundary; text and controls stay sharp. Placement is retained during the current app run.

Switching fields or apps

Recording continues and insertion pauses. Return to the same unchanged field during recording to resume. If you finish elsewhere, the text is saved and waits for the small Insert action. Returning after Finish never triggers delayed automatic insertion.

Keep the original field unchanged until insertion completes. Manual edits, a closed target or unsupported accessibility may require an explicit destination in Review. The app never submits or sends the text.

Clipboard

Every insertion attempt temporarily backs up your current clipboard and returns it automatically, whether insertion succeeds or not. A newer copy you make takes priority. You do not need to manage clipboard recovery.

If Windows temporarily refuses clipboard access, the app keeps the backup and retries its return automatically. Insert becomes available when that return finishes. The explicit Copy text and Copy this original actions put your chosen text on the clipboard for you to use.

Confirmed insertion closes the banner. Otherwise your transcript stays in the banner with Insert. Select the destination cursor and click Insert to make a new attempt with a fresh backup of your current clipboard. The banner stays open until insertion is confirmed or you close it with X; closing keeps your text in History.

Verify after recording and Review

Enable Verify after recording on the Dictation section to reveal the Verification model selector. Choose a different eligible model for a second pass over the audio. Verification takes extra time, and either model can be wrong. Turning verification off hides these details and retains an eligible selected model for next time.

  1. Dictate and stop recording.
  2. If live text was inserted, the transcript is saved before its owned draft is removed from a validated destination. Surrounding text stays. Preview first has no destination draft to remove.
  3. The first transcript stays visible in a read-only banner preview while verification runs. Scroll longer text without opening an editor. With Insert directly, agreement inserts the final result and closes the banner; differences open compact Review. With Preview first, agreement and differences both open Review without inserting.
  4. Click an underlined phrase and choose its correct wording from the two alternatives. The live wording is first; the alternative is second. There are no model labels in the choices.
  5. After each choice, the next unresolved phrase opens automatically.
  6. In an ordinary Insert directly Review, the final choice returns the finished text to the remembered cursor. Preview first and further transcripts keep Review open after resolving choices; use Insert when ready. Saving edited text always keeps insertion separate.

The destination is checked again before insertion. If it changed or cannot be validated, the draft may remain in place and Review keeps your text with an explanation. Use Choose destination when needed. Cancelled or failed sessions do not automatically insert on completion.

Try a second model on the same recording

Select Transcribe again in Review or Details, choose Model and confirm Transcribe in that same window. This uses the protected original recording without opening the microphone or changing your source transcript. The compact Review stays open while the new comparison runs. Even if the models agree, this action waits for explicit placement. An already inserted History item can also be compared this way.

The complete result appears under Alternative transcript. Select Use this transcript to make the entire alternative your saved working text. You can edit it further. The original outputs remain unchanged, and a manual draft replaced by this action remains available in the draft archive in Details. This action does not insert text into another app.

Type directly into the transcript

Click ordinary transcript text, press F2, or select Edit text to edit the whole draft in the compact Review window. Delete an extra word, add punctuation, or rewrite a sentence. Actual edits are saved automatically, including when Review closes.

While editing the whole draft, phrase alternatives pause so they cannot overwrite your corrections. Select Save text to keep the complete result here. A Saved status confirms protected saving; a failure keeps the editable text available for retry. Select the destination cursor and use Insert separately when ready. Typing, saving or pressing Enter never inserts it. Originals and comparison choices remain in Details.

Insertion currently accepts a single paragraph. Pasted line breaks or control characters stay preserved for correction; remove them before inserting. Already inserted transcripts remain read-only.

Review has its own compact window.

No large editor opens automatically. Pending items remain available for later. In the optional Details editor, Close and Copy reviewed text keep the item; Dismiss removes the saved unfinished Review; a retained recording remains in History.

Choose a destination

To place ready text, click in the desired external editable field so its cursor is visible, then click Insert in the floating Review. This captures the cursor you just chose, including after an earlier attempt failed. An exact original live draft at that cursor is replaced; text left at another position is preserved, so check for earlier drafts before choosing a different position.

In Insert directly, automatic Finish uses the original validated field. If you finish while another window is active, the result stays pending. It will not appear later just because focus returns. Preview first always waits for explicit Insert. For an alternative using the shortcut from Details:

  1. Resolve all differences in Review.
  2. Select Choose destination. The banner shows placement instructions.
  3. Click the desired editable field and leave an empty cursor at the insertion point.
  4. Press your configured recording shortcut.

The shortcut now inserts Review instead of recording. Opening Settings or returning to Review cancels placement; choose again to rearm. Check for drafts left elsewhere to avoid keeping two copies.

Manage installed models

Open Models → Installed models. The table shows your local models, their test status and live mode. Actions below the table use the row you select.

  1. Select a row, then Test selected model with audio… to try a local recording.
  2. To uninstall an app-managed model, choose Remove selected model and confirm its name. Shared model files are kept; the reason appears below the table.

Add a model

  1. In Add model, choose an available model or paste a public Hugging Face URL.
  2. Select Check size and compatibility.
  3. Review the download size, then select Download model.

Use Cancel download to stop a download. Closing the window does not cancel it. Installed models stay in the upper table. A different eligible model that has passed an audio test can be selected for Verification.

The small banner language indicator shows the actual result, such as Auto · SK or Auto · EN. Before detection it shows Auto · …. A fixed language shows its code, such as SK or ES.

Under Preferred Auto languages, use + to add a language supported by installed models, or remove a tag with its ×. With tags selected, Auto chooses only among those supported by the selected model, and the banner correction picker offers only your preferred languages. Remove all tags to use and offer every supported language. The initial SK and EN tags exclude Russian, Serbian and Czech. This restriction is not a confidence or accuracy guarantee. The local information icon explains these preferences and possible live-preview delay; the smaller gray qualifier identifies model support.

The small model name beside the phase identifies the Dictation model while recording and the Verification model during Verify. Hover the name to see its full identity and role.

Click the banner language to reprocess the same recording in another language. The choice names any required model switch. While recording, keep dictating and press your usual shortcut to Finish; the new pass then uses the original audio without recording again. Escape also cancels a queued pass. If you change focus after Finish, the result waits for explicit Insert.

After the banner closes, use History → More actions (⋯) → Change language…. This creates a new Review and preserves the previous transcript and edits. It never automatically replaces text already inserted elsewhere. Audio is retained even when recognition returned no text.

KInIT is Slovak-only. For English, use a multilingual model and select en or Auto. Forcing the wrong language can corrupt wording. The local comparison covered three Slovak recordings with user-supplied or corrected reference wording; it is not a universal accuracy ranking.

History and original audio

Move the pointer into the gap between the recording list and transcript, then drag to resize them. The divider line is hidden until you hover or focus it with the keyboard. With the divider focused, use the arrow keys for small adjustments. Narrow windows stack the panes and hide the divider; widening restores your preferred width. The thin scrollbars keep more space for the content.

The record count appears in the selected detail header. Empty History explains how to create the first dictation.

Select History in the main window. History in the notification-area menu opens this same section. The list includes recordings and text marked Needs review. Text-only items remain available even when their original audio is gone.

  • The Play, Pause and Stop icons control the selected original recording. Pause keeps your position; Stop returns to the beginning. The compact player keeps the buttons, timeline and elapsed/total time on one line. Hover for playback state; playback failures remain visible.
  • Drag the round timeline thumb to seek. Elapsed/total time show your position. The faint waveform behind the slider shows RMS energy on a fixed scale; loud isolated peaks do not shrink the rest of the recording. With the slider focused, Left/Right move one second; Page Up/Page Down move five seconds. Selecting a recording does not play it.
  • Copy text copies the current transcript.
  • Open Review opens the item's current text in the separate compact Review window. Select Details for its original wordings and recording information. Reopening an item does not insert it.
  • More actions (⋯) → Save audio… exports an ordinary WAV to a location you choose.
  • The small × beside each row deletes that dictation after confirmation. The prompt identifies its date and time and starts with Cancel selected. Select Delete to confirm. Confirming removes its recording, transcript and saved Review choices. More actions (⋯) → Delete… uses the same confirmation for the selected item.
Recordings are kept for 7 days by default; transcripts stay until you delete them.

Change this in Settings → History → Keep recordings for, or search settings for retention. Choose 1 day, 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, 1 year or Forever. Cleanup removes expired audio and keeps its transcript as a Text only item. Shortening the period can remove older recordings immediately. Cleanup runs while the app is open or next time you open it; busy dictation waits. A longer period cannot restore audio already removed. A recording with captured audio can be retained even when no words were recognized; use Change language… to try that audio again.

Insert a saved pending result

  1. Select the dictation in History and choose Open Review.
  2. Resolve the remaining wording choices, or edit and approve the final draft.
  3. Click the destination field so its text cursor is visible, then click Insert in the floating Review. The Insert click keeps that destination focused.

If an earlier attempt left some live text elsewhere, inspect it before choosing a new position. Explicit Insert replaces a live draft only when its exact ownership at the selected cursor can be proven; otherwise earlier text is preserved. An unconfirmed result can be retried using explicit Insert once automatic clipboard return has finished.

Try another model or correct the language

Choose Open Review → Transcribe again, select an eligible different model in the inline selector and confirm Transcribe. It creates a separate comparison, keeps the source and your edits, and shows the full alternative with Use this transcript. Save text and Insert are separate actions. A saved original recording and an eligible installed, runtime-tested verifier are required; see Review.

Use More actions (⋯) → Change language… in History to transcribe the same audio in another supported language. It creates a new Review without starting the microphone or changing text previously inserted elsewhere.

An item marked Already inserted shows the saved text with insertion and phrase editing disabled. Its original choices and recording information remain available through Details. Use Copy text if you want to reuse that text manually.

Open Review, Copy text and More actions sit beneath the selected text. The player stays below both columns. History list entries show short previews, while the detail uses the available height. At narrower sizes, the list moves above the selected transcript; scroll the page to reach the actions and player. Leaving History or closing the main window stops playback. Returning keeps the selected dictation and does not play it automatically.

History is protected for your Windows account. Exported WAV files are ordinary, unencrypted audio; export recordings you need beyond the limit.

Settings and shortcuts

The Dictation section groups the microphone, model, language, After recording and both live options. Insert live text at the cursor sits directly below Live transcription. Enable Verify after recording to show its additional settings. Verification works with either output choice; it takes extra time and does not guarantee correctness.

Search settings at the upper left, above the named menu, finds settings by name, category, description or keyword. Press Ctrl + F to focus it, then select a result or press Enter. The app opens and scrolls to that setting. Hidden dependent options lead to their prerequisite without changing it. Search also finds recording shortcut behavior with terms such as hold, press or toggle. Press Esc to clear search. Small information icons beside settings explain them on hover, click or keyboard activation; the same text is available to screen readers.

Record above Help in the navigation footer starts a dictation for Review; while recording it becomes Stop. For automatic delivery to an external cursor, start with your recording shortcut in that field. Help stays at the bottom of the navigation panel and is also available with F1.

Open the Settings section for appearance, shortcut, and local service preferences. Recording and connection state appear in the bottom status bar. Automatic saving feedback shares that area on Dictation and Settings; saving failures remain visible on every section. Important global warnings expand above the content. The native title bar identifies the app, leaving more room for your text.

Appearance
System follows Windows at app startup. Light / Dark override it. Preview is immediate and saves automatically. Windows high contrast takes priority.
Recording shortcut

Use Enable in the Keyboard shortcut header. Choose Press to start/stop to toggle recording with each press, or Hold to record to record while the complete shortcut is held and finish when one of its keys is released. The default is Press.

The current combination appears as keycaps. Select the pencil icon to change it, press the new combination and release every key to save. Listening… shows when capture is active. Escape, Tab, the cancel icon or leaving the control abandons the change. A successful save ends Listening automatically. Shortcut editing waits until the current recording or transcription has finished.

Default: Ctrl + Shift. You can also use Ctrl/Shift/Alt with Space, letters, digits or F1–F24. Modifier-only combinations can overlap other applications' shortcuts, especially in Hold mode; a combination with a dedicated key reduces that overlap. Changes made during a held recording apply after its release or cancellation.

History
Keep recordings for controls audio lifetime, with a 7-day default. Transcripts stay until explicit deletion. Expired audio cannot be played, exported or transcribed again. Choose Forever to retain recordings until deletion.
Saving
Valid model, microphone, language, appearance, and toggle changes save automatically. Text settings save after a short pause or focus change. Invalid text shows an error and leaves the previous valid configuration active. During a session, changes apply to the next recording.
Help
F1 or Help at the bottom of the navigation panel opens this local guide in your default browser. Ctrl + F searches; the browser's Print command makes a paper/PDF copy.

Close Settings or exit the app

The title-bar × closes the main window; the app stays available in the Windows notification area. Choose Exit from its notification-area menu to quit.

If a session is active, Exit asks to stop it, then waits while it saves the received text and original recording before closing. Stopping this way keeps the text for Review and does not complete automatic insertion. Captured audio can appear in History even if recognition returned no text. If protected Review saving fails, the app stays open so you can copy or explicitly dismiss the unsaved text.

Local transcription service

The app starts its local transcription service automatically when it opens. An existing listener is preserved, including while its runtime prepares. Wait for Ready · Local transcription before recording; reconnection is automatic. If startup fails, correct the service address or launcher problem and use Start local API to retry. Closing the app leaves the service running.

Current address: http://127.0.0.1:48125. TypeWhisper's separate batch server on port 8123 is not this client's session service.

Troubleshooting

No models appear, or the service is offline
  1. The app starts its service automatically. If it remains offline, open Settings → Local transcription service and select Start local API to retry.
  2. Wait for preparation, then select Reconnect. Confirm the address points to the session service.
  3. Check that the required model is installed on the Models section.
The shortcut does nothing
  1. Check the connection status first. If the local transcription service is offline, open Settings → Local transcription service and select Start local API. Wait for the service to prepare and reconnect. Starting dictation while offline opens Settings without a recording banner.
  2. Check Enable keyboard shortcut and read the saving and shortcut status.
  3. If TypeWhisper is running, the shared default Ctrl + Shift is disabled here. Quit TypeWhisper and use Recheck shortcut, or choose a different shortcut.
  4. A test launch with --no-hotkey disables shortcuts. Relaunch normally. Also check whether another app uses the same key combination.
The microphone is silent, unavailable, or recording is interrupted
  1. Reconnect the microphone and select Refresh microphones on the Dictation section.
  2. Select the intended input device and check the automatic saving status. System default microphone follows the Windows default capture device.
  3. Check Windows microphone access and the input level. Watch whether the banner's waveform reacts while you speak.
  4. Retry after correcting the device or service issue. Check History for text marked Needs review.
The banner records, but no text appears yet

Check whether live transcription is enabled for this model. After-recording profiles return text after Finish. In live mode, initial model loading and processing can delay the first partial. An empty transcript can also indicate silence or the wrong microphone.

The app switched to Review or will not resume at the cursor

If only the beginning of a dictation appeared, the full transcript remains in History with its review status. A live correction must validate the text already inserted before replacing it. A range warning means delivery stopped; inspect the partial text before choosing another destination so it is not inserted twice.

A slow accessibility check can temporarily pause delivery. Once that check has ended, fresh live updates can resume in the same unchanged field. An uncertain insertion is never repeated automatically. The clipboard returns independently; your transcript remains available through Insert.

Return to the original field and leave its text unchanged. The destination must expose reliable editable text and selection access. A protected, elevated, unsupported, closed, or manually edited field cannot be used automatically. Continue through Review and Choose destination.

Record starts from the app window and needs a destination selection. For an automatic final return without live insertion, choose Insert directly, turn off Insert live text at the cursor and start with the shortcut from the destination field. Preview first always waits for explicit Insert.

A wording choice or final insertion could not finish

Resolve every underlined phrase, or select Keep the remaining wording. In an ordinary Insert directly Review, the last choice inserts automatically when the original field is valid. Preview first and further transcripts wait for explicit Insert. If the destination is gone, select a new cursor and use Insert. Reviews reopened after restarting the app need a new destination selection.

Remove line breaks/control characters from a manual final draft. If protected saving fails, keep Review open and correct the storage problem before retrying.

If the app says text was inserted but its saved insertion status could not be updated, check the destination and preserve a copy. Do not insert the same pending item again after restarting. Items marked Already inserted have insertion disabled.

In an ordinary Insert directly Review, automatic completion and the final wording choice use the validated original destination. Preview first and further transcripts wait for explicit Insert, which uses a fresh capture of the external cursor you selected. A click in Review's editor activates Review; click back into the destination before Insert. Unsupported, protected or elevated fields may still refuse placement. Your text stays protected if validation fails.

Some editors temporarily leave pasted text selected. The app waits briefly for the original field's text and cursor to settle; it does not send a second paste while checking. Both must match exactly, apart from the specific Outlook line-break case below. If the field cannot confirm insertion, inspect it before retrying. Compatibility still depends on the destination application's accessibility support.

Dictating into Outlook or another Office editor

Click in the message body so the text cursor is visible, then start with your dictation shortcut. In desktop Outlook with Insert directly, the live draft stays in the recognition pipeline; Finish returns the complete text to that original cursor after processing and any required word choices. Preview first opens Review and waits for Insert. Your signature and surrounding text stay in place. Paste Special is not part of the dictation workflow.

When you start at the beginning of a message above a signature, Outlook may report two leading line breaks as one after the paste. The app can confirm this specific first insertion only when the complete dictated text, the rest of the message, and the final cursor position match. It does not paste the result again.

If an Office paste menu or another small editing popup is open, close it and return the cursor to the message body before starting. A Paste Options button belongs to Office; it is separate from the dictation banner. If insertion is unconfirmed, the clipboard returns automatically and the transcript stays in the banner.

Insertion is unconfirmed, or Windows is busy with the clipboard

If Insert cannot validate the destination, Review shows the failure while keeping your edited text. A Windows access-check failure includes its numeric error code. Select the destination field and try Insert again; keep the code if the problem repeats.

The transcript stays in the banner with Insert. Your clipboard returns after every attempt; it does not wait for the destination to confirm. Check whether text already arrived before retrying so that you do not insert it twice. Insert uses your newly selected external cursor and a fresh clipboard backup.

The app briefly checks an unchanged original field without sending text again. If it can confirm the existing insertion, the banner closes. Otherwise it stays until you retry successfully or use X. X hides the banner and keeps your protected text in History.

If another application locks the clipboard, Local Dictation retains the complete backup and retries restoration automatically. A newer copy takes priority. Settings shows the temporary error without opening another window. A genuine restoration failure can briefly prevent another insertion or normal Exit; an unconfirmed insertion alone does not.

A very slow destination can process its queued paste after the clipboard has returned and read the restored contents. Automatic return cannot completely prevent that case. The temporary dictation payload has a one-second deadline even if the destination's accessibility check stalls.

A model repeats the same word many times

Whisper can get stuck repeating a word during recognition. The current original Whisper profiles retry suspiciously repetitive segments against the audio using their native generation fallback. This does not delete repeated words from your text and cannot guarantee a correct transcript.

Large-v3 can take longer to finish when it retries. Progress remains visible; Esc cancels. If a result is still wrong, correct it directly in Review. Keep the original recording in History for comparison.

Very short or quiet fragments can also produce invented greetings or thanks. The current live method avoids isolated quiet fragments and uses the complete original recording for its final primary pass. That improves context but cannot guarantee every word. If a model unexpectedly returns empty text after a received draft, the app preserves the preceding draft for Review instead of replacing it with a blank result.

Why is live mode unavailable for a model, or slow with Large-v3?

The built-in Large-v3 profile now supports live transcription, with potentially slow updates. Its earlier restriction was a catalog policy, not a limitation of Whisper itself. New imports still start without live capability. If Large-v3 remains disabled, reconnect to an updated local session API so the app receives its current capabilities.

Local Dictation is already running

Open the existing app from its Windows notification-area icon instead of launching another copy. The notification-area menu also offers Settings, History, and Exit.

A recording is missing from History or will not play

History keeps transcripts until you delete them. Recordings expire after the period chosen in Settings → History, including recordings where no words were recognized. A missing recording is shown as Text only; earlier versions could also remove items under the old count limit. Check your playback device if a saved item will not play. A pending Review can outlive its history audio; it is not an unlimited recording archive. If audio saving fails, the transcript remains as a Text only item in History.

Local files and privacy

Recording and speech transcription run locally. The client sends audio to its loopback session service, not to a cloud transcription provider. Installing a model can contact Hugging Face to download its files. This Help page has no network dependencies or tracking.

Data locations can differ in a custom installation. History metadata, history audio, and unfinished text are protected with Windows DPAPI for the current user. Keep that account and its recovery arrangements in mind when backing up or moving data. Settings and explicit audio exports are ordinary files.

Cursor insertion uses a temporary paste while preserving supported previous clipboard formats. Dictation payloads are marked to stay out of Windows clipboard history and cloud synchronization. Unsupported clipboard formats stop delivery before changing the clipboard.
