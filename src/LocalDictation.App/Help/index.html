Help · works offline

Speak. Check. Keep writing.

Record speech, watch the local transcript, and return reviewed wording to the field where you started.

F1 opens Help from Settings. Ctrl + F searches this guide in your browser.

Start dictating

Open Local Dictation from the Windows notification area. Wait for Ready · Local transcription, then choose your microphone and model. Valid settings changes are saved automatically; check the saving status before starting.

Write directly at your cursor

  1. Enable Insert live text at the cursor on the Dictation tab. The change is saved automatically.
  2. Click your destination field, with no text selected.
  3. Press and release your shortcut, Ctrl + Shift by default. Speak.
  4. Press the shortcut again, or select Finish in the banner, to stop.

Keep live text in the banner

Turn off Insert live text at the cursor, then start with the shortcut from your destination field. The setting saves automatically.

The app remembers that cursor while live text stays in its own banner. After normal completion and any required word choices, it returns the finished text automatically.

Record in banner starts from the app window without an external cursor. Use Choose destination when the text is ready.

The app inserts text; it does not send it.

Sending, submitting, and saving the destination remain your actions.

Recordings last up to 10 minutes. During a session, Esc cancels recording or verification. Cancellation or an interrupted recording does not automatically insert received text; it stays available for review.

With verification off, normal completion inserts directly into your remembered field. Review appears when words need a choice or insertion needs attention. Agreement between the two models also returns the text automatically.

Live text and the banner

Live transcription shows partial wording in the banner. With Insert live text at the cursor enabled it also updates a valid destination as you speak. With that option off, a shortcut-started recording remembers the original field and inserts the final text after normal completion and any required choices. Updates arrive after speech segments and processing, and can be revised. Without live mode, wait for the result after Finish.

In desktop Outlook, live wording stays in the banner. After Finish, the complete result is inserted once at the original valid cursor, after any verification choices. Live recognition stays on, and your live-insertion preference still applies to other apps.

Live wording is a draft. The app waits for enough speech context, then refreshes it roughly every few seconds of new audio when the model can keep up. After Finish, the same dictation model checks the complete original recording before returning the final text. This happens with Verify after recording off too; verification adds a separate model and word choices.

Finishing can revise earlier wording. Keep the original field unchanged until insertion completes. The Turbo profiles usually finish much sooner than Large-v3 on this PC; Large-v3 may need tens of seconds. Initial runtime preparation and model loading add time. Progress stays visible and Esc cancels.

Move or collapse the banner

  • Drag the microphone tile, title/status area, or waveform to move the banner away from your text.
  • Hide text collapses the recording preview. Show text opens it again. Word choices remain visible during compact Review.
  • Banner controls preserve your destination cursor.
  • Manual position is kept for the current app run, including later recordings, but not across restarts.

What happens when you click elsewhere?

Recording continues; insertion pauses. Return to the same unchanged field to resume at the draft's end, even if you click inside it.

A changed field needs a new decision.

Manual edits or a closed destination prevent automatic replacement. Your transcript stays in Review. Use Choose destination when needed.

Insertion requires reliable Windows accessibility access. Protected, elevated, or unsupported fields fall back to Review.

Verify after recording and Review

Enable Verify after recording on the Dictation tab to reveal the Verification model selector. Choose a different eligible model for a second pass over the audio. Verification takes extra time, and either model can be wrong. Turning verification off hides these details and retains an eligible selected model for next time.

  1. Dictate and stop recording.
  2. The transcript is saved before its live draft is removed from a validated destination. Surrounding text stays.
  3. Wait for comparison to finish. Review stays inside the small recording banner.
  4. Click an underlined phrase and choose its correct wording from the two alternatives. The live wording is first; the alternative is second. There are no model labels in the choices.
  5. After each choice, the next unresolved phrase opens automatically.
  6. The final choice returns the finished text to the remembered cursor. If no differences need a choice, normal completion returns the text automatically.

The destination is checked again before insertion. If it changed or cannot be validated, the draft may remain in place and the banner keeps your text with an explanation. Use Choose destination when needed. Cancelled or failed sessions do not automatically insert on completion.

Type directly into the transcript

Click ordinary transcript text, press F2, or select Edit text to edit the whole draft in the same banner. Delete an extra word, add punctuation, or rewrite a sentence. Actual edits are saved automatically, including when Review closes.

While editing the whole draft, phrase alternatives pause so they cannot overwrite your corrections. Select Use edited text when ready to return it to the validated destination. Typing or pressing Enter never approves it. Originals and comparison choices remain in Details.

Automatic delivery currently accepts a single paragraph. Pasted line breaks or control characters stay preserved for correction; remove them before approval. Already inserted transcripts remain read-only.

Ordinary Review stays in the banner.

No large editor opens automatically. Pending items remain available for later. In the optional Details editor, Close and Copy reviewed text keep the item; Dismiss removes pending Review. Retained History is separate.

Automatic insertion accepts single-paragraph text. Remove added line breaks/control characters before insertion. Rejection leaves the field unchanged and keeps the text available.

Choose a destination

Use this when no external cursor was captured, for older Reviews after restart, or when the original field is no longer valid. A preview-only recording started with the shortcut from a valid field already has a remembered destination.

  1. Resolve all differences in Review.
  2. Select Choose destination. The banner shows placement instructions.
  3. Click the desired editable field and leave an empty cursor at the insertion point.
  4. Press your configured recording shortcut.

The shortcut now inserts Review instead of recording. Opening Settings or returning to Review cancels placement; choose again to rearm. Check for drafts left elsewhere to avoid keeping two copies.

Models and languages

Choose Dictation model and Language on the Dictation tab; valid choices save automatically. The dropdown also lists catalog entries marked not installed; install those before selecting them for recording. Available languages and verification choices depend on the model.

Install or test a model

  1. Open the Models tab.
  2. Use Inspect selected catalog model, or enter a public repository URL/owner/name and Inspect compatibility and size.
  3. Check the size, then Download inspected model.
  4. Select the installed Dictation model and Test selected model with audio….

Imports support compatible built-in Whisper Transformers models with original safetensors; private, gated, and remote-code models are rejected. New imports start after-recording. Closing Settings does not stop downloading; use Cancel download.

A note below Dictation model appears when it needs a runtime test, is missing, works after recording only, or may have slow live previews. Support and measured low-latency readiness are separate. The three named Turbo/Large-v3 profiles support live; other newly imported profiles still start without live capability.

Installed, runtime-tested, quality-checked, and live-ready are separate states. A successful runtime test proves the model can transcribe; check its accuracy and live performance separately. Verification needs a different, installed, runtime-tested model supporting your language.

KInIT is Slovak-only. For English, use a multilingual model and select English or Auto. Forcing the wrong language can corrupt wording. The local comparison covered three Slovak recordings with user-supplied or corrected reference wording; it is not a universal accuracy ranking.

History and original audio

Open History from the notification-area menu, or select Open transcript and audio history on the History tab.

  • Play audio plays the selected original recording; Stop stops playback.
  • Copy text copies the current transcript.
  • Open Review opens the item's current text in the compact banner. Select Details for its original wordings and recording information. Reopening an item does not insert it.
  • Save audio… exports an ordinary WAV to a location you choose.
  • Delete removes the selected history transcript and audio after confirmation.
History keeps the latest 20 dictations with audio.

The next saved item removes the oldest transcript and audio. Pending Review is separate. Recordings without received text do not create history items.

An item marked Already inserted shows the saved text with insertion and phrase editing disabled. Its original choices and recording information remain available through Details. Use Copy text if you want to reuse that text manually.

History is protected for your Windows account. Exported WAV files are ordinary, unencrypted audio; export recordings you need beyond the limit.

Settings and shortcuts

The Dictation tab groups the microphone, model, language, and both live options. Insert live text at the cursor sits directly below Live transcription. Enable Verify after recording to show its additional settings.

Open the Settings tab for appearance, shortcut, and local service preferences. Connection and automatic saving status share one row at the top of the window.

Appearance
System follows Windows at app startup. Light / Dark override it. Preview is immediate and saves automatically. Windows high contrast takes priority.
Recording shortcut
Enable the shortcut and check the automatic saving status. Default: Ctrl + Shift. Alternatives: Ctrl + Alt + Space, Ctrl + Shift + D, Alt + F9. Use Ctrl/Shift/Alt with Space, letters, digits, or F1–F24.
Saving
Valid model, microphone, language, appearance, and toggle changes save automatically. Text settings save after a short pause or focus change. Invalid text shows an error and leaves the previous valid configuration active. During a session, changes apply to the next recording.
Help
F1 or Help (F1) in the Settings window opens this local guide in your default browser. Ctrl + F searches; the browser's Print command makes a paper/PDF copy.

Close Settings or exit the app

Close closes Settings; the app stays available in the Windows notification area. Choose Exit from its notification-area menu to quit.

If a session is active, Exit asks to stop it, then waits while it saves the received text and original recording before closing. Stopping this way keeps the text for Review and does not complete automatic insertion. A recording needs received text to appear in History. If protected Review saving fails, the app stays open so you can copy or explicitly dismiss the unsaved text.

Local transcription service

If offline, use Start local API, wait for preparation, then Reconnect. Disconnected clients also retry automatically.

Current address: http://127.0.0.1:48125. TypeWhisper's separate batch server on port 8123 is not this client's session service.

Troubleshooting

No models appear, or the service is offline
  1. Open Settings → Local transcription service and select Start local API.
  2. Wait for preparation, then select Reconnect. Confirm the address points to the session service.
  3. Check that the required model is installed on the Models tab.
The shortcut does nothing
  1. Check Enable keyboard shortcut and read the saving and shortcut status.
  2. If TypeWhisper is running, the shared default Ctrl + Shift is disabled here. Quit TypeWhisper and use Recheck shortcut, or choose a different shortcut.
  3. A test launch with --no-hotkey disables shortcuts. Relaunch normally. Also check whether another app uses the same key combination.
The microphone is silent, unavailable, or recording is interrupted
  1. Reconnect the microphone and select Refresh microphones on the Dictation tab.
  2. Select the intended input device and check the automatic saving status. System default microphone follows the Windows default capture device.
  3. Check Windows microphone access and the input level. Watch whether the banner's waveform reacts while you speak.
  4. Retry after correcting the device or service issue. Check Pending Review for text already received.
The banner records, but no text appears yet

Check whether live transcription is enabled for this model. After-recording profiles return text after Finish. In live mode, initial model loading and processing can delay the first partial. An empty transcript can also indicate silence or the wrong microphone.

The app switched to Review or will not resume at the cursor

If only the beginning of a dictation appeared, the full transcript remains in the banner. A live correction must validate the text already inserted before replacing it. A range warning means delivery stopped; inspect the partial text before choosing another destination so it is not inserted twice.

Return to the original field and leave its text unchanged. The destination must expose reliable editable text and selection access. A protected, elevated, unsupported, closed, or manually edited field cannot be used automatically. Continue through Review and Choose destination.

Record in banner starts from the app window and needs a destination selection. For preview-only dictation with an automatic final return, turn off Insert live text at the cursor and start with the shortcut from the destination field.

A wording choice or final insertion could not finish

Resolve every underlined phrase, or select Keep the remaining wording. The last choice inserts automatically when the original field is valid. If that destination is gone, use Choose destination. Reviews reopened after restarting the app need a new destination selection.

Remove line breaks/control characters from a manual final draft. If protected saving fails, keep Review open and correct the storage problem before retrying.

If the app says text was inserted but its saved insertion status could not be updated, check the destination and preserve a copy. Do not insert the same pending item again after restarting. Items marked Already inserted have insertion disabled.

Some editors temporarily leave pasted text selected. The app waits briefly for the original field's exact text and cursor to settle; it does not send a second paste while checking. If the field cannot confirm insertion, inspect it before retrying. Compatibility still depends on the destination application's accessibility support.

Dictating into Outlook or another Office editor

Click in the message body so the text cursor is visible, then start with your dictation shortcut. In desktop Outlook, the live draft stays in the banner; Finish returns the complete text to that original cursor after processing and any required word choices. Your signature and surrounding text stay in place. Paste Special is not part of the dictation workflow.

If an Office paste menu or another small editing popup is open, close it and return the cursor to the message body before starting. A Paste Options button belongs to Office; it is separate from the dictation banner. If insertion is unconfirmed, inspect the message and follow clipboard recovery below before retrying.

Paste is unconfirmed, or the previous clipboard needs recovery

Open the destination and inspect whether text arrived. Do not retry blindly: a slow editor may still consume a queued paste. The app keeps the dictation paste available and retains your previous clipboard in memory.

After inspecting the destination and stopping any deferred paste, select Restore clipboard… in the idle banner, or Restore previous clipboard… from the notification-area menu. Confirm only after the destination can no longer consume the earlier paste. Recording and finishing must stop before restoration is available.

The banner's X hides the idle recovery message and keeps your saved text and clipboard backup. A new recovery event or an explicit Start/Exit attempt can show it again. New delivery and normal Exit still wait for recovery. A newer copy made in another app is preserved. Avoid force-closing the app while it holds the backup; a crash or forced termination loses that in-memory state.

A model repeats the same word many times

Whisper can get stuck repeating a word during recognition. The current original Whisper profiles retry suspiciously repetitive segments against the audio using their native generation fallback. This does not delete repeated words from your text and cannot guarantee a correct transcript.

Large-v3 can take longer to finish when it retries. Progress remains visible; Esc cancels. If a result is still wrong, correct it directly in Review. Keep the original recording in History for comparison.

Very short or quiet fragments can also produce invented greetings or thanks. The current live method avoids isolated quiet fragments and uses the complete original recording for its final primary pass. That improves context but cannot guarantee every word. If a model unexpectedly returns empty text after a received draft, the app preserves the preceding draft for Review instead of replacing it with a blank result.

Why is live mode unavailable for a model, or slow with Large-v3?

The built-in Large-v3 profile now supports live transcription, with potentially slow updates. Its earlier restriction was a catalog policy, not a limitation of Whisper itself. New imports still start without live capability. If Large-v3 remains disabled, reconnect to an updated local session API so the app receives its current capabilities.

Local Dictation is already running

Open the existing app from its Windows notification-area icon instead of launching another copy. The notification-area menu also offers Settings, Pending Review, History, and Exit.

A recording is missing from History or will not play

History contains the latest 20 items that had both received text and recorded audio. Earlier items may have been removed by the limit or explicitly deleted. Check your playback device if a saved item will not play. A pending Review can outlive its history audio; it is not an unlimited recording archive. If History saving fails, the transcript is kept in Pending Review, but audio recovery is unavailable.

Local files and privacy

Recording and speech transcription run locally. The client sends audio to its loopback session service, not to a cloud transcription provider. Installing a model can contact Hugging Face to download its files. This Help page has no network dependencies or tracking.

Current Windows installation
WhatTypical location
ApplicationC:\Git\Local-Dictation\app\LocalDictation.exe

Data locations can differ in a custom installation. History metadata, history audio, and pending Review are protected with Windows DPAPI for the current user. Keep that account and its recovery arrangements in mind when backing up or moving data. Settings and explicit audio exports are ordinary files.

Cursor insertion uses a temporary paste while preserving supported previous clipboard formats. Dictation payloads are marked to stay out of Windows clipboard history and cloud synchronization. Unsupported clipboard formats stop delivery before changing the clipboard.
